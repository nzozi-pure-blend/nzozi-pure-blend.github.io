ROOTED IN NAKIVALE, GROWING FOR UGANDA

More Than Juice, We Are Nzozi

In the heart of Nakivale, Isingiro, Uganda, NZOZI PURE BLEND works hand-in-hand with local farmers and refugees to turn surplus fruit into pure, nutrient-rich juice. We partner with smallholder farmers to source ripe mangoes, passion fruit, and citrus that would otherwise go to waste, paying fair prices and reducing post-harvest loss. Our production is powered by trained youth from the Nakivale community, creating steady jobs and skills in food processing, packaging, and quality control. Every bottle supports livelihoods, promotes sustainable agriculture, and strengthens community resilience.

FROM FARM TO BOTTLE IN NAKIVALE

Clean Process, Honest Work

Our work starts in the fields and ends in your hands. Local farmers in Nakivale grow fresh mango, orange, pineapple, passion fruit, and herbs. We select only ripe fruits, wash them carefully, blend them fresh, and pack every bottle in our clean production room in Nakivale. We ensure hygiene, quality, and freshness at every step. We create jobs, train youth, and add value to Ugandan agriculture.
